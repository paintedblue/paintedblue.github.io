ICTC 2023

ChatGPT Powered Digital Healthcare System

Digital healthcare Prompt engineering Smart devices

Background

A conversational chatbot responds to the information a person provides, but ongoing health support also requires context about symptoms, activities, and changes over time. Unstructured inputs and lengthy responses can make this information difficult to use.

We explore a connected approach to chronic pain support, bringing together patient-reported symptoms, smart-device data, and structured ChatGPT prompts to inform monitoring and feedback.

How It Works

  • Collect and organize inputs. The proposed input module combines reported pain symptoms with biometric and motion data from connected devices.
  • Structure model responses. Prompts specify tasks, rules, and JSON output formats for organizing generated condition information, management suggestions, and precautions.
  • Connect monitoring with feedback. Monitoring is designed to identify changes in activity or physiological state, request context-specific outputs, and present them through smartphones, wearables, or other smart devices.

System Demonstration

We developed an architectural proposal and demonstrated structured prompts through example inputs and generated outputs. These examples show how information could flow across modules for patient input, generated condition information, monitoring, data storage, and feedback.

The demonstration focuses on system integration and response structure. Evaluation with users and clinical validation remain future work.

What We Learned

Explicit tasks, response rules, and output examples provide a way to organize model responses for use across connected system modules. The architecture also highlights the importance of linking feedback to the person’s current context.

Two challenges remain: interpreting sensor data to recognize activities and choose appropriate feedback timing, and validating the medical information generated by ChatGPT. Addressing both is necessary before evaluating the system in patient care.
