KoBot 1.5 Improvement Research

Statistical AI services Prompt engineering Question-refinement flows

Overview

KoBot 1.5 is an improvement project for Statistics Korea’s generative AI chatbot. We proposed interface redesigns, prompt templates, and question-refinement flows to help users find relevant statistical tables. The work was conducted by the Statistical Service Innovation Task Force during the 2025 1st Youth Internship Program.

Background

The team’s work on building and curating KoBot training data informed the improvement proposal. Internal-user feedback, hands-on observations, and survey results revealed repetitive answers, hallucinated resources, date recognition errors, and difficulty discovering related statistics.

Users also had trouble distinguishing statistics recommendation from general AI chat. These issues motivated clearer guidance, more structured responses, and clarification of ambiguous questions.

Design & Development

Interface Design

  • Redesigned the main screen to clarify that KoBot specializes in recommending statistical tables.
  • Added concepts for monthly popular statistics and related resource recommendations.
  • Presented Figma screens for the main page, result page, term explanation panel, help and FAQ, and newly proposed recommendation features.
  • Modified the response format to include a key summary, representative figures, and the context or purpose of the recommended statistics.
  • Separated terminology explanations into a sidebar-style flow to reduce confusion.

Prompt Design

  • Developed user-facing question guides by analyzing responses across question types, including time expressions, comparative or choice questions, and keyword-sensitive queries.
  • Created prompt templates for question refinement, including target_augmentation_template and time_normalization_template.
  • Proposed system-side clarification flows for ambiguous targets, age groups, relative dates, and statistical keywords.

My Role

I led UI/UX design, translating user needs into feature concepts and interaction flows for the chatbot. I also contributed to prompt-template improvements and presentation materials.
