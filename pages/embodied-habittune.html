HabitBuddy

Under review · First author

Child–AI interaction Personalized media Family routines

Overview

HabitBuddy explored how personalized AI-generated songs and character videos could support preschoolers and parents in everyday routines. Building on HabitTune, the project focused on children's engagement with personalized media in family life.

My Role

As first author, I led the research from study design through execution, analysis, and synthesis.

  • Research design. Framed the research questions and developed the overall research approach.
  • Study design and execution. Designed the study procedures and conducted the study.
  • Data analysis. Analyzed the collected data to address the research questions.
  • Synthesis and documentation. Brought the findings together and organized the research into a coherent account.
