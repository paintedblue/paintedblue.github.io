HabitBuddy

Under review · First author

Child–AI interaction Personalized media Family routines

Background

Everyday routines such as toothbrushing, tidying up, and preparing for bed help young children develop self-care and self-regulation. Yet getting started often depends on repeated parental reminders, which can become nagging and create stress for both parents and children.

HabitBuddy explores how personalized media can make these moments easier and more enjoyable. Building on HabitTune, it combines AI-generated songs, a character that demonstrates the activity, and achievement stamps to support children's participation in daily routines.

How It Works

  • Personalized songs. Parents and children share the target behavior, barriers, a familiar routine cue, and the child's interests. AI-generated lyrics and melodies turn these inputs into a song featuring the child's name and preferences.
  • A routine companion. A squirrel character demonstrates the target behavior alongside the song, giving children actions to imitate and a sense of doing the activity together.
  • Achievement stamps. Stamps awarded after completing the activity provide visible feedback on participation.

In-Home Study

We studied HabitBuddy with 14 families of children aged 3–6 over two weeks of use and a one-week follow-up. Parent questionnaires, daily reports, usage logs, and interviews with parents and children helped us understand how the system fit into family life.

During follow-up, families were asked to discontinue regular use but could replay songs at their child's request, allowing us to explore whether songs had become an expected part of their routines.

What We Learned

Children enjoyed hearing their names and interests in the songs. Parents reported easier routine initiation and participation, along with less nagging, routine-related stress, and conflict. In some families, children began anticipating or requesting the songs themselves.

Changes in behavioral performance varied across activities and measures. The findings highlight how personalized enjoyment, behavioral guidance, and achievement feedback can work together to support family routines.

My Role

I led the design and evaluation of HabitBuddy, focusing on experiences for young children and their families.
