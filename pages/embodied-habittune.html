HabitBuddy

Under review · First author

Child–AI interaction Personalized media Family routines

Overview

I developed and studied HabitBuddy to explore how personalized media can support preschoolers and their parents during everyday routines. Building on my earlier work on HabitTune, I combined AI-generated songs with character videos to create experiences around activities such as toothbrushing and tidying up.

My research focused on how children engage with these experiences and how they fit into parent–child interactions at home. I was interested in how design choices could support children's participation while responding to families' everyday needs.

My Role

As first author, I led the research from framing the questions and designing the study to conducting it, analyzing the data, and synthesizing the findings.

  • Research Conceptualization & Study Design. I framed the research questions and designed the study methodology and procedures.
  • Study Execution & Data Collection. I conducted the study and collected the data needed to address the research questions.
  • Data Analysis & Interpretation. I analyzed the study data and interpreted the findings in relation to the research questions.
  • Research Synthesis & Reporting. I brought the findings together, articulated their implications, and organized the research for communication.
