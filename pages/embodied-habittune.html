HabitBuddy

Child–AI interaction Personalized media Family routines

Overview

HabitBuddy combined personalized AI-generated songs and character videos to support preschoolers and parents in everyday routines. Building on HabitTune, the project explored how personalized media could support children's participation in daily activities.

My Contribution

I led the design and evaluation of HabitBuddy, focusing on experiences for young children and their families.
