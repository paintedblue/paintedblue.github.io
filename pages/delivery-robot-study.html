Understanding Human–Robot Interaction in Commercial Delivery Services

Under review · Co-first author

Human–robot interaction Delivery services Everyday interaction

Overview

This project examined how people encounter and engage with robots in everyday delivery services. Drawing on data from a real-world service, it explored human–robot interaction in the context of everyday behavior.

My Role

As co-first author, I focused on qualitative data analysis and developing research insights.

  • Qualitative analysis. Analyzed delivery service data to understand people's interactions with robots.
  • Thematic analysis. Identified and organized recurring patterns in the data into themes.
  • Insight development. Synthesized the analysis into insights about human–robot interaction in everyday settings.
