Understanding Human–Robot Interaction in Commercial Delivery Services

Under review · Co-first author

Human–robot interaction Delivery services Everyday interaction

Overview

I studied how people encounter, interpret, and interact with delivery robots in everyday service settings. Working with data from a commercial robot delivery service, I examined these encounters from the perspective of the people using and sharing spaces with robots.

My focus was on understanding human behavior in context: how people make sense of a robot's presence, respond during an interaction, and navigate the practical demands of receiving a delivery. Through qualitative analysis, I sought to inform the design of more understandable and usable robot services.

My Role

As co-first author, I contributed qualitative and thematic analysis, along with the interpretation and synthesis of findings.

  • Qualitative & Thematic Analysis. I analyzed the service data, identified recurring patterns, and developed themes describing people's interactions with robots.
  • Interpretation & Synthesis. I interpreted the themes in context and synthesized them into research insights about everyday human–robot interaction.
