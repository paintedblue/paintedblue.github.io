WhiteCane

Accessibility research Robot-mediated navigation App development

Overview

WhiteCane is a campus navigation project for visually impaired users at Ewha Womans University. As the mobile interface for a navigation robot, the app brings together destination search, outdoor route guidance, and a transition to indoor navigation.

Background

Campus journeys extend from outdoor paths into buildings. WhiteCane explores how a mobile interface can support this journey through voice input, spoken guidance, and a connected flow between outdoor and indoor navigation.

Design & Development

Key Features

  • Search campus destinations by text or voice and review the selected location on a map.
  • Provide outdoor route guidance with live location tracking, remaining distance, and spoken navigation updates.
  • Transition to an indoor guidance interface on arrival, with destination selection and waypoint-based route displays.
  • Offer controls for voice guidance, speech speed, and vibration preferences.

Implementation

The mobile app was built with Flutter, integrating map and routing services, speech recognition, and text-to-speech. The indoor guidance interface was prepared for integration with the team's routing algorithm, which remained a separate development task.

My Role

I led UI/UX design and developed the mobile app for the navigation robot. My work covered destination search, voice interactions, and the transition between outdoor and indoor guidance.
