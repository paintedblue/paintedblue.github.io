A Guideline-Based Approach for Creating Personalized Storybooks with Generative AI

Personalized books Generative AI Human–AI collaboration

Background

Generative AI makes it easier for parents to create stories that reflect their child's age and interests. However, a personalized draft still needs careful review for age-appropriate language, narrative coherence, and consistent illustrations.

This study explores how revision guidelines can support parents as they shape AI-generated content. It examines the complementary roles of AI as a generator and parents as reviewers who understand their own children.

How It Works

  • Generate a personalized story. AI creates an initial story from the child’s age and preferences. Parents review and revise the text before generating illustrations.
  • Review text and images. Separate guidelines help parents check narrative structure, language, personalization, visual details, and alignment between illustrations and the story.
  • Assemble the storybook. A custom web platform combines the finalized text and images into an e-book for parents to review.

User Study

Twenty parents of toddlers participated in a remote study comparing storybook creation with and without revision guidelines. The study targeted primary caregivers of children aged 12–36 months.

Both groups followed five stages: text generation, text revision, image generation, image revision, and final review. We examined what parents changed, the challenges they encountered, and how useful they found the guidelines.

What We Learned

Parents remained essential for checking developmental appropriateness and adapting stories to their children. Text revisions addressed coherence, language, and personalization, while image revisions focused on visual errors, consistency, and missing details.

Guidelines helped parents notice issues and make more specific revisions, but they also added review effort and sometimes repeated information already captured in prompts. The findings point toward flexible, example-based guidance that supports quality without overwhelming parents.
