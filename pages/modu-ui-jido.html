Map for All: Barrier-Free Campus Map

Accessibility research Barrier-free navigation Campus mobility

Overview

Map for All is a barrier-free campus navigation app for wheelchair users at Chungnam National University. It combines accessible routes, facility locations, and building information to help users plan their movement around campus.

Background

Wheelchair users on campus face route constraints that general map services do not represent well: steep slopes, stairs, inaccessible building entries, missing elevators, and changing construction conditions.

Design & Development

Key Features

  • Search campus buildings and view accessible facility information.
  • Display ramps, accessible restrooms, convenience facilities, rest areas, and custom places on a map.
  • Route around steep roads, alleys, and stairs by assigning higher path costs to wheelchair-unfriendly segments.
  • Support construction reports and detailed building information by floor.

Implementation

The project combined campus building category data, directly created facility location data, and OpenStreetMap. The app followed an MVVM structure with clear separation between UI, business logic, backend, and database layers.

My Role

I led project planning and UI design and contributed to frontend development. I also helped organize development documentation and team collaboration practices.

Outcome

🎉 Grand Prize at the Chungnam National University Public Data Utilization Competition.
