About Me

I'm an M.S. student in Computer Engineering at Ewha Womans University, working in the HCI Lab with Prof. Uran Oh.

My research explores how AI and robots shape people's behavior in everyday life. I developed HabitTune (CHI EA '26), which creates personalized AI-generated songs to help children build daily habits, and later extended this work through HabitBuddy, which combines personalized songs and character videos to support preschoolers and parents in their daily routines. I also study how people accept and engage with robots in everyday settings, drawing on data from a real-world robot delivery service.

In my work with children, I've observed how even small design choices can quickly influence their behavior. Going forward, I aim to understand how AI and robots can be designed to support young children's development.
